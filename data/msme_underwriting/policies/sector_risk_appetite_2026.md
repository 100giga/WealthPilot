# Sector Risk Appetite Note 2026

> Synthetic note written for the underwriting pilot; illustrative only.

## Risk Tiers
| Tier | Sectors (NIC 2-digit) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Food processing (10), pharma & chemicals (21), IT services (62), clinics & diagnostics (86) | Standard terms |
| Medium | Textiles (13), plastics & packaging (22), fabricated metal (25), auto components (29), wholesale trade (46), logistics (49) | Standard terms |
| High | Construction contractors (43), retail trade (47), restaurants & catering (56) | DSCR approval level +0.10; pricing +0.50% |
| Negative | Tobacco products (12), real estate development (41) | Not financed (KO_NEGATIVE_LIST_SECTOR) |

## Rationale
- Construction contractors carry long receivable cycles (often more than
  100 debtor days) and depend on government payments.
- Restaurants and retail trade show high cash-deposit shares, which limits
  how far bank statements can be relied on.
- Auto components: watch concentration on one OEM. Ask for the top-customer
  share and treat anything above 60% as a concentration flag in the credit
  memo.

## Seasonality
Textiles, retail and food processing peak in October and November around
the festive season. Assess working-capital limits on peak-season
requirements, not on the monthly average.
