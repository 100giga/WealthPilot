# MSME Pricing Grid 2026

> Synthetic pricing grid written for the underwriting pilot; illustrative only.

## Benchmark
All MSME loans are priced off the External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR),
taken in this pilot as 8.85% p.a. The rate is reset quarterly when the
benchmark changes.

## Spread by Risk Grade
| Grade | Spread over EBLR | Indicative rate |
|---|---|---|
| WP1 | 1.50% | 10.35% |
| WP2 | 2.00% | 10.85% |
| WP3 | 2.50% | 11.35% |
| WP4 | 3.25% | 12.10% |
| WP5 | 4.00% | 12.85% |
| WP6 | 5.00% | 13.85% |
| WP7 | 6.25% | 15.10% |
| WP8 | Not eligible | — |

## Adjustments
- High-risk-tier sector: +0.50%.
- Fully secured by residential or commercial property: −0.25%.
- The CGTMSE guarantee fee is charged separately and is not included in the
  rate.

## Disclosure
The sanctioned rate, all fees and the annualised percentage rate must be
shown in the Key Fact Statement before the borrower signs.
