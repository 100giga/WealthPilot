# CGTMSE Credit Guarantee: Product Note (Illustrative)

> Summary written for the underwriting pilot. Guarantee percentages and fees
> change periodically; confirm them against the current CGTMSE circulars.

## What It Covers
The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)
guarantees collateral-free credit to eligible MSMEs. Lenders must not take
collateral security or a third-party guarantee on a CGTMSE-covered
facility.

## Eligibility in This Pilot
- Collateral type is "none_cgtmse".
- The sanctioned amount is up to Rs 10 crore (the ceiling was raised from
  Rs 5 crore with effect from 1 April 2025).
- The borrower is not in a negative-list sector and has no knock-out flags.
- Retail trade is eligible.

## Guarantee Cover (Indicative)
- Micro enterprises with facilities up to Rs 5 lakh: 85%.
- Women-led enterprises, and facilities in the North-East region: 85%.
- All other eligible facilities: 75%.

## Fees
The annual guarantee fee is charged on the outstanding amount and depends
on facility size and the borrower's risk. The fee is passed through to the
borrower and is quoted separately from the interest rate.
