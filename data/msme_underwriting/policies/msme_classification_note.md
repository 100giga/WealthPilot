# MSME Classification Note (Illustrative)

> Summary written for the underwriting pilot. Verify against the current
> Gazette notification before using it in production.

## Classification Thresholds (Revised, Effective 1 April 2025)
An enterprise is classified on both investment in plant and machinery (or
equipment) and annual turnover. If either measure exceeds a category's
limit, the enterprise moves to the next category.

| Category | Investment up to | Turnover up to |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Rs 2.5 crore | Rs 10 crore |
| Small | Rs 25 crore | Rs 100 crore |
| Medium | Rs 125 crore | Rs 500 crore |

Before 1 April 2025 the limits were Rs 1 / 10 / 50 crore (investment) and
Rs 5 / 50 / 250 crore (turnover).

## Udyam Registration
Every MSME borrower must hold a Udyam Registration Number (format
UDYAM-XX-00-0000000). The category shown on the Udyam certificate is
self-declared and updated from ITR and GST data. Where the category we
derive from financial statements differs from the Udyam certificate, the
borrower must update the registration (CND_UDYAM_CATEGORY_UPDATE). Priority
sector classification follows the derived category.

## Exports
Export turnover is excluded from turnover when determining the category.
