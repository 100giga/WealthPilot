# Collateral and Security Guidelines

> Synthetic guidelines written for the underwriting pilot; illustrative only.

## Collateral-Free Lending
Loans to micro and small enterprises up to Rs 10 lakh must not require
collateral. Larger collateral-free facilities are covered by CGTMSE (see
the CGTMSE product note).

## Accepted Security and Loan-to-Value
| Collateral type | Maximum loan-to-value | Valuation |
|---|---|---|
| Residential property (equitable mortgage) | 65% | Empanelled valuer, every 3 years |
| Commercial property | 60% | Empanelled valuer, every 3 years |
| Hypothecation of stock and book debts | 75% of drawing power | Monthly stock statement |
| Hypothecation of machinery | 70% of invoice value | Invoice and insurance |
| Lien on fixed deposit | 90% | Not applicable |

## Drawing Power
For Cash Credit, drawing power is (paid stock + receivables under 90 days)
less margin. Receivables older than 90 days are excluded.

## Insurance
All hypothecated and mortgaged assets must be insured, with the lender
noted as loss payee.
