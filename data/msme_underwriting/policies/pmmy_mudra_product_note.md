# PMMY Mudra Loans: Product Note (Illustrative)

> Summary written for the underwriting pilot; verify against current
> scheme guidelines.

## Loan Categories
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) loans fund non-farm income-generating
micro enterprises:

| Category | Loan amount |
|---|---|
| Shishu | up to Rs 50,000 |
| Kishore | Rs 50,001 to Rs 5 lakh |
| Tarun | Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh |
| Tarun Plus | Rs 10 lakh to Rs 20 lakh, for borrowers who have repaid a Tarun loan |

## Underwriting Relaxations
- No collateral. Facilities are covered under the credit guarantee scheme.
- The minimum-vintage knock-out is waived for Shishu and Kishore loans, so
  new enterprises may apply.
- New-to-credit promoters are accepted without a third-party guarantor.
- Tenure is 36 to 60 months.

## Standard Checks Still Apply
DSCR, banking conduct, bureau knock-outs, KYC and AML screening apply in
full. A Mudra loan does not bypass compliance.
