# NIFTY 50 Index Fund — Fact Sheet (2026)

## Overview
The NIFTY 50 Index Fund tracks the NIFTY 50 index, investing in the 50 largest
companies listed on the National Stock Exchange of India. It is a passively
managed, low-cost equity fund suitable for long-term wealth accumulation.

## Fees & Expenses
The total expense ratio (TER) is 0.20% per annum, charged daily and reflected
in the NAV. There is no entry load. Exit load is 0.5% if redeemed within 7 days
of purchase, and nil thereafter.

## Risk Profile
This fund carries equity market risk and is classified as "Very High" on the
riskometer. It is appropriate for investors with a moderate-to-aggressive risk
tolerance and an investment horizon of 5 years or more.
