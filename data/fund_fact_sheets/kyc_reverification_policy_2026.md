# Client KYC Re-Verification Policy (Effective 2026)

## Scope
Applies to all retail advisory clients whose Know Your Customer (KYC) record
is more than 24 months old, or whose risk category is "high".

## Current Rule
Clients in the "moderate" or "low" risk category must re-verify KYC every
24 months. Clients in the "high" risk category must re-verify every 12
months. Re-verification may be completed fully online via video KYC; an
in-person visit is no longer required as of this policy version.

## Escalation
If a client's KYC has lapsed by more than 90 days, advisory services must be
suspended until re-verification is complete. This does not apply to
existing SIP (systematic investment plan) instructions already in force.
