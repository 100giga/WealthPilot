# Gold ETF — Fact Sheet (2026)

## Overview
The Gold ETF invests in physical gold of 99.5% purity, held in secure vaults,
and is listed on the exchange. Each unit approximately represents 1 gram of
gold. Fund code: GOLD-ETF-INV-2026-0417.

## Fees & Expenses
The total expense ratio (TER) is 0.65% per annum. There is no exit load.

## Risk Profile
Classified as "Moderately High" on the riskometer. Gold prices can be
volatile in the short term but historically act as a hedge against inflation
and currency depreciation. Suitable as a 5-15% allocation within a
diversified portfolio, not as a primary holding.
